SellForCash is an Indian buyback service designed to provide a quick, secure, and hassle-free way to sell used, old, or damaged electronics, specializing primarily in laptops, MacBooks, and gaming PCs. The process is fully automated online: users select their device brand and hardware specifications to generate an instant price evaluation, after which they can schedule a free doorstep pickup. During the pickup, a field technician inspects the hardware, performs a verified, secure data wipe, and issues immediate payment via UPI, bank transfer, or cash on the spot. Operating across major metropolitan hubs—including Mumbai, Pune, Delhi, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad—the company handles everything from individual pre-owned devices to dead units for scrap and corporate IT inventory liquidation.
