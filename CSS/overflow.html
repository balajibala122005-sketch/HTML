This is a simple paragraph to demonstrate overflow behavior.A semantic tag can be either inline or block-level, depending entirely on the specific element you choose. "Semantic" simply means the tag describes its own meaning to the browser and developer, while "inline" and "block" describe how that tag physically behaves on the page layout. To fix text or paragraph overflow in CSS, use overflow: hidden, text-overflow: ellipsis, and white-space: nowrap for single lines, or use overflow-wrap: break-word to wrap long words inside a container. Most users on Webflow Forum agree that combining these properties effectively truncates excess text.
